# 📋 Template Coverage Matrix

Tasmota GPIO Templates for IoTextra mezzanine modules across all IoTsmart & IoTbase platforms.

> **Key principle: The Template is selected by the carrier board, not by the SoM.**
> GPIO pin assignments are identical for all SoMs installed in the same carrier board.
